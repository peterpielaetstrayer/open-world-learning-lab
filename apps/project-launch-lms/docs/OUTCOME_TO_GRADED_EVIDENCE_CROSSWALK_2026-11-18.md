This is deliberately stricter than a superficial keyword crosswalk.

## Smallest defensible decision for Peter and Dr. Evans

### Recommended for faculty review: retain foundational MLOs and explicitly assess a tiny, shared-case evidence strip

Keep the existing learner-owned Direction Brief and Launch Portfolio as authentic CLO-level transfer. In addition, propose **three short pieces of shared-example evidence per module** that are explicitly *submitted and scored* (either a compact appendix to an existing graded assignment or a separate low-stakes graded foundational check). Reuse the cases already taught; do not invent a full additional project.

**Module 1 graded foundation strip (draft, not live):**

1. Name two interests/questions that could initiate projects.
2. For three supplied project ideas, explain relevance, feasibility, and scope in a concise table.
3. Use the supplied focus checklist to mark one broad/focused contrast and give a one-sentence reason.

**Module 2 graded foundation strip (draft, not live):**

1. Label purpose, milestones, resources, constraints, and intended evidence in Maya's supplied plan.
2. Show the completed first-action checklist for the supplied Ari scenario with one justification.
3. State one evidence-based conclusion, one thing the evidence **cannot** establish, and how feedback changes (or justifiably does not change) the next decision in the worked case.

To count, each prompt must appear in the actual submitted graded artifact (or expressly graded foundational assessment) **and the assessor must score it against a clearly identified descriptor**. Simply adding a link to ungraded lesson answers does not solve the issue. A separate foundational check would introduce an additional graded item; embedding the strip would change existing submission requirements and could require rubric points to be apportioned. **Neither is authorized yet**. Estimate workload and run a 15-minute student pilot before making either mandatory.

### Alternative requiring explicit faculty consent: revise MLO contexts

Ask whether objectives may be written to cover a supplied *or learner-generated* plan. This could use existing graded transfer evidence, but risks repeating the too-broad CLO-level performances that prompted Dr. Evans's original comment. **Do not make this change unilaterally.**
