## Creative direction
Make short authored explanatory films, not generic motivational montages. OWLL editorial learning studio aesthetic: warm paper #f6f2e8, deep green #19342e, teal #235965, sage #dce8dc, warm ochre #9a6a35 for tradeoffs, restrained serif headings and sans-serif body. Realistic diagrams of fictional artifacts, no personally identifiable learners, no real campus layouts. Signal decisions with labels and shapes as well as color. Keep essential information in the narration or accessible transcript. Use one focal idea per scene, generous margins, minimal moving text; no flashing or distracting music. Prefer human narration recorded by Peter if desired, no imitation of another voice. Human-edited script/captions and source ownership documentation. 16:9 master; 1080p production target, browser-friendly H.264/AAC, separate accurate WebVTT and full descriptive transcript. Keep caption area free of essential labels. No fabricated learner evidence or promises of outcomes.
