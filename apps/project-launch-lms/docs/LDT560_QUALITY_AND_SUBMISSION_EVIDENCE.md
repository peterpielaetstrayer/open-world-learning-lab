## Assessment evidence for LA 5.1

- **Rubric attached to Module 4 graded work:** Launch Portfolio, 60 possible points. Criteria cover purpose and planning coherence; resources, constraints and feasibility; first action and evidence interpretation; and feedback, revision and next decision. Four performance levels 15, 10, 5 and 0 per criterion. The 30-point Direction Brief supplies supporting applied-skill evidence.
- **Kirkpatrick Level 2:** the ten-item scored scenario quiz measures knowledge; Direction Brief and Portfolio measure applied project reasoning; Your Next Move asks learners about their confidence, willingness to revise and next-step intentions. Self-report is interpreted as attitude evidence.
- **Kirkpatrick Level 1:** ten experience rating questions plus one optional response. Survey responses are for course improvement and separate from assessment scores.
